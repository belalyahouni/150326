  Notes

  - OLMoE has 64 experts total (8 active per token). Cache size 64 = all experts fit on GPU,
  essentially no-offload but with overhead → explains ~1.8x slowdown vs baseline.
  - Cache size 12 is very aggressive (only ~19% of experts on GPU). 4x slower than full-cache
  expert offload, ~8.5x vs baseline.
  - Full CPU offload (vllm's --cpu-offload-gb) is the worst at ~8x slower than baseline.
  Expert-offload with cache-size 12 is actually faster than full CPU offload, suggesting the
  expert cache is doing useful work.
  - TTFT scales roughly proportionally with TPOT across configs — the bottleneck is uniformly
  expert loading latency, not a prefill-specific issue.
  - The gradient from cache-size 64 → 12 → full-CPU shows clear sensitivity to how many experts
  fit on GPU, which is exactly the regime the unified pool idea targets.
